Privacy summary

We do not sell personal data because we do not collect personal data for our own use.

1. Scope

{{organizationName}} is a company based in {{companyLocation}}.

This policy applies to our website and the applications listed in our catalog.

An app-specific notice takes priority if a future app needs different data to provide its stated function.

2. How our apps handle data

Our apps process your files on your device. We do not receive your photos, documents, edits, or export files.

Our apps do not require an account. They do not send telemetry, crash reports, or usage statistics to us.

Your device may back up or synchronize files through services that you enable. Those services follow their own privacy policies.

3. User-requested network access

Some features can download optional fonts, frames, or other public resources when you request them.

The resource provider receives normal network information, such as your Internet Protocol address. We do not receive or retain that information.

We identify each external source and its license inside the applicable app.

4. Website hosting

Cloudflare delivers this website and our theme files. Cloudflare must process basic network data to answer a web request.

This data can include an Internet Protocol address, request time, browser details, and security signals.

We do not add Cloudflare Web Analytics or use request data for behavioral profiles or advertising.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies. It does not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.

The theme control saves your light, auto, or dark preference in local browser storage.

This preference stays on your device. We do not receive or use it to identify you.

Links can take you to external websites. Those websites control their own storage, logs, and privacy practices.

6. Messages that you send

If you contact us, we receive the information in your message. We use it only to reply and resolve your request.

Your email provider and our email provider process the message under their own terms.

7. Children

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

8. Policy changes

We will update this page if an app or the website changes its data practices.

The effective date shows when the current policy took effect.
