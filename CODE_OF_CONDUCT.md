# Code of conduct for rapid

## Scope and expected conduct

This project-specific policy takes effect on 23 October 2026.
It applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related maintainer communications.
It does not change the maintainer-only contribution policy.

Treat people respectfully and discuss technical claims through evidence.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose another person's private information, or repeatedly disrupt project work.
Do not publish private allegations or personal information in an issue.
Follow maintainer requests that keep discussions relevant and respectful.

## Reporting and enforcement

Owner: Outcrop Inc, WA, USA.
Send conduct reports privately to admin+security@outcrop.us.
Include the relevant links, dates, observed conduct, and any immediate concern.
Share only personal information that the reviewer needs to assess the report.

The owner reviews reports and limits access to maintainers who need the information for that review.
The reviewer may request more information and consider each person's account before deciding what action is appropriate.
Actions can include a private request, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent participation ban.
The reviewer should explain the action and its scope to affected participants when appropriate.
The project does not promise a fixed response time.

To request reconsideration, reply through the same private address and explain the disputed facts or new evidence.
The owner will seek a different reviewer when one is available and has no conflict of interest.
Report software vulnerabilities through [the security policy](SECURITY.md).
